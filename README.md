## 📊 Featured Projects

### [Malaysia Car Registrations & EV Market Analysis](https://github.com/chooromeo/malaysia-ev-analysis)

**PostgreSQL · SQL · Power BI · Power Query · DAX**

An end-to-end analysis of Malaysia's car registrations and battery electric vehicle (EV) market, comparing **January–September 2025 with the same period in 2026** using publicly available registration data.

- Imported registration datasets into **PostgreSQL** and used SQL transformations and aggregations to prepare reporting data for monthly EV and brand/model analysis.
- Built an interactive **Power BI report** covering registration trends, EV adoption, market share, and brand and model performance.
- Compared equivalent nine-month periods to measure year-over-year changes.
- Identified **98.82% growth in EV registrations**, from **26,928 to 53,537**, and a **3.97 percentage-point increase in EV market share**.
